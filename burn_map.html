Before you use these numbers

  • Nothing here has been checked on the ground yet. The scars come from satellite images alone.
  • Scars are a lower bound. A field burned and ploughed between two clear images, a very small field, or straw burned in a heap leaves no scar the images can see.
  • The latest week is never complete. Burns under cloud or smoke show up only on the next clear image.
  • "Possibly burned" land is left out. Fields that darkened but not enough to be sure are not counted; a check on a clear image found that most of them were not burns.
  • Farmland is ESA WorldCover 2021 cropland. In Lahore City and Lahore Cantt it is a small share of mostly built-up land, and scars there are less certain.
  • One square kilometre is the smallest unit on this page, on purpose. Each cell mixes many fields, so the map shows where burning happens without pointing at any one farm.
  • Tehsil outlines are geoBoundaries (ADM3) and some have changed since. Tehsils partly outside the images count only the part inside.
Data: Sentinel-2 L2A and ESA WorldCover 2021 (Microsoft Planetary Computer; contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data), VIIRS active fires from NASA FIRMS (near real time for 2026, standard processing for 2025; persistent hot spots such as kilns removed), tehsil outlines from geoBoundaries (CC BY 4.0).
